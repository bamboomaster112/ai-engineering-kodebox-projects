## Plans
- **Free**: 2,000 build minutes per month and 1 concurrent job.
- **Team**: $15 per user per month, 10,000 build minutes per month and 5 concurrent jobs.
- **Enterprise**: custom pricing from the sales team, with SSO, longer retention and 24/7 support.

## Build minutes
A build minute is one minute of a small hosted runner. Medium and large runners use minutes at 2x and 4x. Self-hosted runners use no build minutes. Unused minutes do not roll over to the next month.

## Overage
On the Team plan, minutes beyond the monthly allowance cost $0.008 each. Free organizations cannot buy extra minutes; their pipelines pause until the next month.

## Invoices
Invoices are emailed to billing contacts on the first day of each billing cycle and are also available under **Organization settings > Billing**.
