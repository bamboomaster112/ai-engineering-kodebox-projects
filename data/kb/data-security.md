---
## Data residency
When you create an organization you choose where its data is stored: the EU region or the US region. The region cannot be changed after the organization is created. To move regions, create a new organization and migrate your projects.

## Encryption and retention
Data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.2 or newer. Job logs are kept for 90 days.

## Compliance
Brightwave CI holds a SOC 2 Type II report. Enterprise customers can request it under NDA from their account manager.
