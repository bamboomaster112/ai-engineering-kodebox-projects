---
## Cancelling
You can cancel a paid plan at any time from **Organization settings > Billing**. Monthly plans stay active until the end of the current billing month and then move to Free.

## Refunds
Annual plans can be refunded on a prorated basis if you cancel within 30 days of purchase or renewal. Monthly plans are not refunded. Build minutes, including overage minutes, are not refundable.

## Requesting a refund
Open a support ticket with your organization name and invoice number. The billing team replies within two business days.
