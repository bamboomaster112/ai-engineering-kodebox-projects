## Channels by plan
- **Free**: community forum only.
- **Team**: email support with a first response within one business day.
- **Enterprise**: email, chat and phone support, available 24/7.

## Enterprise response targets
Priority 1 (production down): first response within 1 hour, any time of day.
Priority 2 (major feature broken): within 4 hours.
Priority 3 (questions and minor issues): within one business day.

## Opening a ticket
Include your organization name, the project, a link to the failing pipeline and what you expected to happen. Never paste secrets or tokens into a ticket.
